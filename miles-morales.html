LEGO Miles Morales Dorm Room

I recreated Miles Morales’ dorm room from Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse using LEGO, combining Blender, Mecabricks, and Stud.io to design and render the full scene. The goal was to capture the atmosphere, lighting, and personality of Miles’ space while translating it into a LEGO format.
