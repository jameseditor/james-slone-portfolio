Art Battle Ottawa

Feb 14, 2022

I spent the night at Art Battle Ottawa on February 14 - yes, Valentine’s Day. Because when you’re single, what else are you supposed to do? The event was an absolute blast. Watching artists transform blank canvases into full paintings in just 20 minutes was electric, and the energy in the room never dipped.

I moved through the crowd with my camera, capturing the action, the details, and the atmosphere - and yeah, I definitely looked pretty cool doing it. Anyway, take a look at some of my favorite shots from the night.
