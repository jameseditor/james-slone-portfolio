Generic Stud Videos

Generic Stud is a LEGO YouTuber that makes comedy videos focused around Ninjago.

How Much LEGO Can You Buy for $100?

Budget videos are always super relatable and this one’s no different for any LEGO fan. Generic Stud cracked a lot of jokes and found some crazy good deals, and I made sure the editing backed that up. I focused on clarity with on-screen prices, meme cuts, B-roll, and transitions that keep the viewer engaged without overwhelming the story. The goal? Make it easy to follow, fun to watch, and visually clean.

Ninjago Minifigure Collection

Generic Stud has a crazy Ninjago collection, honestly I’m kind of jealous.

Throughout this video, I focused on using music that matched the vibe of each minifigure. For example, I used dark, intense tracks when Generic showed off the villain figures. It was really fun to play with such a wide variety of music, it made the whole project super fun.
