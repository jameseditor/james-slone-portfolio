Living In a Gym Until I Get Kicked Out

Since it's my first one, I know I’ll look back one day with a hundred ideas on how to improve it but honestly, I’m proud of what I pulled off. It taught me how to adapt to a completely different style than I’m used to, and that’s incredible.
